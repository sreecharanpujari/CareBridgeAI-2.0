1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
The Healthcare Crisis: Post-Discharge Breakdown & Clinical Hazards
🏥 Systemic Post-Discharge Breakdown

💰 $17B Readmission Burden: Nearly 1 in 5 elderly Medicare patients suffer avoidable hospital readmission within 30 days due to post-discharge confusion.

⚠️ 50%+ Medication Errors: Over half of medication errors happen at home in the first 14 days from double-dosing or skipped blood pressure pills.

📑 3 to 8 Pages of Latin Jargon: Discharge summaries are saturated with complex acronyms (PRN, BID, exacerbation) that bewilder non-medical families.

✍️ Critical Handwriting & Ambiguity Hazards

⚡ Hurried Doctor Cursive: Prescriptions in rapid cursive script and blue ink are unreadable to patients and non-specialists.

❌ Legacy OCR Breakdown: Traditional OCR (Tesseract) drops cursive words completely, outputting blank lines or random noise.

🗓️ Conflicting Clinic Dates: Doctor clinic notes say return Oct 21, but discharge summary says Oct 24—patients miss checkups.

❓ Missing Timings: 'Take 1 tablet daily' without stating Morning or Bedtime triggers dangerous crashes.

🛑 The Critical Safety Gap: Patients leave hospitals with fragmented paperwork, unreadable prescriptions, and zero proactive warning alarms to prevent fatal mistakes at home.

🛡️ The Confusion Detector (Safety Scanner)

Proactively audits medical instructions before discharge to catch hazards:

HIGH SEVERITY Illegible Medicine: Catches notes saying 'Take 1 capsule twice daily' with blurred names. Flags as UNVERIFIED to prevent ingestion.

MEDIUM SEVERITY Missing Timing: Flags drugs with dosage (50mg) but no Morning/Night time slot to avoid hypotensive crashes.

MEDIUM SEVERITY Conflicting Clinic Dates: Flags date mismatches (e.g. Oct 21 vs Oct 24) and provides 1-click doctor contact link.
